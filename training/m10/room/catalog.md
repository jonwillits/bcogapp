# Catalog of openly licensed model sources for Lab 10's dorm room

Gathered 2026-10-25 for Part 3 (spec §7.1). Archives were downloaded to `~/Documents/Projects/bcogapp_data/m10/room/` (outside the repo, outside Box) and only the chosen GLBs and each kit's color texture were copied into `public/m10/room/`, credited in its `NOTICE` and listed in `manifest.json`. `measure.mjs` reads each shipped GLB's bounding box into `sizes.json`.

## Kenney (www.kenney.nl) — CC0 1.0 Universal; used

Each kit page (`https://kenney.nl/assets/<kit>`) links a zip whose `License.txt` reads "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ … free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects. Support us by crediting Kenney or www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)". Kenney's current kits ship a GLB per model with one shared `Textures/colormap.png` referenced by relative path, so a kit's models and its colormap must stay in one folder; the 2018 Furniture Kit embeds its colors in materials and references no texture.

## Quaternius (quaternius.com) — CC0; not used

The pack pages (Furniture Pack, Survival Pack, Junk Food Pack, RPG Essentials, and others) link their licence to https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/, but each download is a Google Drive folder behind a dialog, which cannot be fetched with a plain request. Not pursued, since the Kenney kits covered the room.

None was shipped; the ids above are the place to look if a single distinctive item is wanted later.

## What the room gets from the kits, and what it does not

Furniture, always present: single bed with its pillow, desk, desk chair, open bookshelf, a closed bookcase with doors standing in for a wardrobe, a wall with a window, a plain wall, a front doorway with its door, a rectangular rug, a side table. (A curtain is not in any kit; the scene draws one from primitives if wanted.)

Fitting a dorm room (23 shipped, choose about 12): desk lamp, mini fridge, coffee mug, water bottle, stack of books, laptop, trash can, ceiling fan, standing coat rack, potted plant, speaker, skateboard, pizza box, extra pillow, radio, keyboard, computer screen, mouse, microwave, toaster, cereal bowl, open cardboard box, kick scooter. **Missing from every kit**: backpack, headphones, alarm clock, phone charger, slippers, electric kettle, calculator, notebook, pencil cup, game controller, guitar, toothbrush, hair dryer, poster (primitives where wanted).

Not fitting (22 shipped, choose about 12): shovel, axe, pickaxe, garden hoe, hammer, bucket, candelabra, glass lantern, hay bale, frying pan, workbench with an anvil, wooden barrel, campfire pit, wooden cart, iron fire basket, tent, stone urn, wooden chest, sled, cooking pot, cart wheel. **Missing from every kit**: garden trowel (the hoe or shovel is the nearest), fire extinguisher, baby's rattle, violin bow, traffic cone, fishing rod (only a campfire fishing stand), watering can, hard hat, snow shovel (a plain shovel ships), bowling ball, horse saddle, life jacket, birdhouse, stop sign, tennis racket, oar, pitchfork, bird cage, chainsaw, wheelbarrow (a cart ships), umbrella. Primitives where wanted.
