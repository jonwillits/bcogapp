**Part 2, the learners:** Cut at the dips: *This learner counts. ## 3. The models, as they shipped

- **Part 1's classifier** (`sounds/classifier.ts`): 400 inputs (40 bands × 10 frames of 10 ms, from 20 ms before the burst, bands pooled from the wide-band spectrogram up to 5 kHz, scaled from 60 dB below the patch's peak to the peak), 12 sigmoid hidden units (width 0.25), 3 sigmoid outputs; Lab 7's rule, Φ at the outputs the Teacher setting, Φ at the hidden units passed back; rate 0.02, 12 passes, weights from ±0.05, chosen once before any measurement; 377 ms per training in Node.
- **Part 2's learners**: cut at the dips (`stream/learners/dips.ts`), with its score the mean transitional probability inside a string; the simple recurrent network (`stream/learners/srn.ts`), 12 inputs plus 12 context units, 12 hidden tanh-free sigmoid units of width 0.25, 12 outputs, rate 0.1, one online pass, context starting at 0.5 (Elman, 1990, p. 182), its score minus the mean squared prediction error on a string's second and third syllables with the context reset; **PARSER** (`stream/learners/parser.ts`; Perruchet and Vinter, 1998), one store of units with weights that starts as the 12 syllables at weight 1, takes in the stream in percepts of 1 to 3 shaping units drawn by the seed (p. 250), where a shaping unit is the longest unit at or above the threshold 1 that matches (p. 252, note 2); a percept of one unit adds 0.5 to it; a percept of 2 or 3 units that is already a unit adds 0.5 to it and to each component; otherwise it is created at 1 and each component gains 0.5; then every unit loses 0.05 and every unit sharing a syllable with the percept loses 0.005 more, the unit just created taking neither and the units that gained taking no interference (Table 1, p. 252); a unit at 0 is removed, and the 12 syllables are maintained at no less than 1 (p. 253). One time step reproduces Table 1 to the last digit (`parser.test.ts`). Its score under the spec's rule is a string's weight as a unit, or 0 (§6.3); under the paper's rule the word is chosen if it is a unit above 1, else at random (p. 256; `scoreTestByRecognition` in `stream/test.ts`). Both rules are scored at every run and the student switches between them (§4.2, §8 item 15).
- **Part 3's models** (`words/models/`): guess and check with one stored object per word and α; tallying with β; each fitted by a grid search (41 values; 50 runs per value) to the student's accuracy over occurrences 2 to 5; 200 runs at the fitted value.
- **Part 4's predictors**: the count model (`predictors/counts.ts`, window 1 to 8, weight window − distance + 1, before and after separate or summed, the corpus as one stream with end marks); prediction from counts (`predictors/ngram.ts`); a transformer of 2 layers, 2 heads, width 32, positions 32, feed-forward 128, 29,941 parameters; Elman's simple recurrent network with 64 tanh hidden units, 10,997 parameters; both trained with AdamW at 0.001, batch 32, 20,000 updates on the corpus's first 9,000 sentences, the last 1,000 held out (63.5% of the held-out sentences also occur among the training sentences, since the grammar is small); 8 snapshots at 0, 50, 200, 500, 1,000, 3,000, 10,000, and 20,000 updates; the runtime (`predictors/runtime/seqNet.ts`) matches PyTorch within 1e-4 on 34 prompts' probabilities and attention weights.
