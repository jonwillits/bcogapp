## Log

- **2026-09-01** — **Module 2 unblocked.** §6's separability requirement is unsatisfiable in this engine and was retired rather than met; Part 3 now asks which populations do the same *job*. The argument is two lines of algebra — an inhibitory approacher reverses whenever intensity exceeds `bias/|w|` and steers by `|w| ×` the intensity *difference*, the same scale — and 108 measured configurations agree with it. Worth not rediscovering: **reversal is how a 3a approaches**, so every remedy that suppressed it (clamping reverse, a bias floor, a weaker or a raised light) destroyed approach instead; and the last live candidate population, Q16@2400, was an artifact of a scan whose columns could not say whether a population *goes anywhere*. Four replacement criteria, one definition shared by both engines, each verified to go red by mutation. Two bugs fell out of taking "what a viewer sees" literally: the strongest divergence perturbation described a light at a height that exists nowhere in the scene, and a light under a creature could not be right-clicked away — which is the light a student most wants to remove, since the population swarms it. The new measures and the calibration method are in `separability.ts`; the searches that closed each option are kept as probes.

- **2026-08-31** — **Module 2 built** (Phase 5), on the branch `m02-evolution`, 26 commits, unmerged. The engine went from discrete generations to a continuous life cycle and the food model changed twice; both were Jon's calls and both survived measurement. - **2026-07-16** — Terminology: **"sensor gain" → "connection strength"**, **"base drive" → "actuator bias"** (Jon's call). "Gain" is defensible in control terms (a vehicle *is* a proportional controller), but in NN lingo gain means an activation-function slope, not the strength between two units — and our parameter is unambiguously a **weight** (`actuator = bias + Σ(weight × sensor)`). Since this same map becomes a network in M4/M5/M7, a vocabulary switch at that seam is expensive; "connection strength" is jargon-free for M1 and maps 1:1 onto "weight" later, and "actuator bias" pairs the creature word with the network word so nothing gets retracted. Renamed in the sim too (`strength`, `bias`, `SensorimotorWeights.bias`). Also fixed a self-inflicted wart: the panel showed **two names for one quantity** (slider "Sensor gain" 2.40 vs readout "Connection strength" −2.40) — the redundant readout is gone, and the slider now displays the *signed* value that appears in the equation while its position still sets magnitude (sign belongs to the phenotype: a 3a with a positive weight just *is* a 2a).
- **2026-07-16** — Inspector: **live numbers + per-vehicle tuning**.
