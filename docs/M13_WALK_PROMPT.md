Record his decisions in the walk-through's per-part *Notes:* lines and under §9's items as they come, not at the end. The spec is his to revise.
- Give him, per part, only what no test or script already establishes: how a screen reads to a student who has not seen the part, whether the instructions are clear, pacing and feel, legibility on the one dark theme, and how the sounds sound. Every item says what to press before what appears. Cross each list against the config's claims and the test files first.
- Bundle updates: a change his answers call for that does not touch later parts goes on a to-do list until it blocks the next stage; a wording rule that recurs is applied over every screen at once, then `texts.probe.ts` is rerun and notes §1a replaced.
- If a change moves a number: rerun the probe that measures it (section 7), then correct notes §4, the walk-through's ranges and the test bands. Never retune a mechanism to make a number come out. Numbers are findings, not targets. No parameter from memory: a model whose paper is not in the library stays *Reference pending* (PARSER).
- Keep the suite green (1,326 tests, about 45 s alone) and `npm run closeout m13-language` clean (5 NO TEST claims expected). Run them one after the other in the background, writing to the scratchpad; concurrent runs produce spurious timeouts.
- The build's non-negotiables, each held by a test, must survive every fix: commit-then-reveal on rendered markup in every part (`reveals.test.tsx`); no held-back word before its reveal and neutral tab names (`naming.test.ts`); every browser-learned weight through Lab 7's `learnWeights` and only the classifier and the recurrent network call it (`oneWriter.test.ts`, `structure.test.ts`); the learners read only syllables; no microphone, camera, POST, dialog or `Math.random` (`privacy.test.ts`); storage only through the shared layer under `bcogapp.m13.v1`; no person, face, vocal-tract drawing or recording of a child; no simulated student or class; the runtime within 1e-4 of PyTorch (`agreement.test.ts`); the WAV decoded by the lab's own parser (never `decodeAudioData`, which resamples).
- Writing rules (Jon, 2026-11-09, in `LAB_COMMON_FEATURES.md` §8 and `naming.test.ts`): no agentic verb for anything that is not a person, including the app, a tab, a screen, a panel and the handout (*shows*, *says*, *prints*, *records* are fine); literal verbs; instruction text in the student's terms; US spelling; typographic quotes; every term defined; what every number counts. A selected control changes color and never size; live text never reflows; every text selectable; a large counter on every timed screen.

## 5. How the walk goes

Jon goes part by part in his browser and writes what he sees and hears. For each message of his:

1. Reproduce what he reports in the pane, or in a test, before changing anything.
2. Put his answers in the feedback file and the walk-through as they come.
3. Fix the batch, run the affected tests and probes, commit once with the reason.
4. Report what changed and what he should now see, in a few lines; a screenshot only for a visual change.

Doing Parts 1, 2 or 3 himself is not a naive run and he knows it; he does them for the mechanics and the pacing. *Clear my saved results* on the Open tab resets everything and draws a new seed behind an in-page confirmation.

**What only he can judge, and the session cannot:** every sound. The pane plays audio to no one. Whether the continuum runs *b* to *d* to *g*, whether the vowels sound like *ee, ah, oo*, whether the stream is even, whether Kokoro's voices are clean, whether the cursor sits on the sound by ear. Ask, do not assert.

## 6. Driving the scene from the pane

- There is no 3D view and no sim clock; the panels render while the pane is hidden, but take a screenshot only with the pane shown (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane {pane: "preview"}`).
- Buttons: `button.click()` from `javascript_tool` is reliable; a `find` ref's click sometimes lands nowhere. Keys are synthesized as `KeyboardEvent`s on `window` (`useKeys` listens for `B`, `D`, `G`, `1`, `2`).
- Audio: in DEV the player is `window.__m13player` (`now()` gives the audio clock; `stop()`), and the most recent cursor position is `window.__m13cursor`. Timed blocks stop on a hidden page or lost focus (`useBlockGuard`), so a block cannot be driven from a hidden pane; show it first.
- To jump to a later screen, build a saved state in the page: `await import('/src/sim/language/progress/state.ts')` (Vite serves TS), make a fresh state, fill a unit (`p1-labels`, `p1-labeling`, `p1-abx`, `p2-minute`, `p2-board`, `p3-words`) as complete with a result of the shape the tests' fixtures use (`reveal.test.tsx` exports them), write it to `localStorage['bcogapp.m13.v1']` and reload. A module edit under HMR leaves the old store instance running, so string changes in a store show only after a reload.
- Timing numbers come from `node scripts/m13-timing.mjs`, never from the pane.
