## 3. The models, as they shipped

**The tendency unit** (`foragers/tendency.ts`): one `RateUnit` with one weight per log (ten), all starting at 0, saturating at ±6; when the forager digs at log i the input is one-hot at i and the prediction p = the sigmoid (Lab 7's width 0.25) of the weight is made and held; when the outcome arrives, a = 1 for beetles and 0 for nothing, Φ = a − p through `thirdFactor('prediction', …)`, Δbᵢ = η · xᵢ · Φ with η = 0.5, written by `learnWeights` and nothing else. Its state is a fixed number of numbers (the weights, the one-hot input, the held prediction, the last Φ); `structure.test.ts` holds it to no list that grows, no timestamp, no identifier naming a record, and `oneWriter.test.ts` walks `src/sim/memory/` and the scene. The Tendency-only forager digs the log with the highest weight among those not dug tonight, ties broken by its seeded stream, so it digs smooth logs until their weights fall.

**The fast store** (`foragers/store.ts`). The input is the night's features as a one-hot vector (3 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 13) and a context of 12 dimensions, a unit vector weighted 2 against the features' four ones, that drifts each night by a step 1.3 of its length and is renormalized (cosine between one night's context and the next about 0.6). **The separation stage** is Lab 7's *Fixed at random* units with *Sparse*, not a second version: `randomDirection` and `sparseBaseline` are now exported from `src/sim/fish/brain.ts` and the fish's `randomUnit` calls them; the units' weights have length 2.5 and the sigmoid Lab 7's width; the sparse baseline is set against 200 random nights. The slider's three settings: **weak** 8 units, half active; **medium** 60 units, about one in seven active; **strong** 480 units, about one in thirty active. A night is stored once, at dawn, as its code with the logs emptied that night. **Retrieval**: the cue is encoded, each stored night's share is the fourth power of the cosine between its code and the cue's, normalized to sum to one, and the answer is the blend of their contents. The forager's cue for last night is what last night was like, its features and the context as it was then, which it holds as one register overwritten at each dawn. **The forager acts on the retrieved strength**: a log the store puts at 0.5 is treated as emptied last night half the time, by a draw from the forager's stream, with no threshold written in (`structure.test.ts`); the map answer does the same, marking *last night* that often and otherwise *two nights ago* (or *not emptied* on night 2). Every forager with knowledge of kinds digs in a seeded order among the logs it treats as worth digging; only Tendency only's order is its weights'.

**The slow learner** (`foragers/slow.ts`): 13 inputs (ten things, all present from the start, and three relations) → 8 hidden units (sigmoid, width 0.25) → 36 property outputs (sigmoid); starting weights uniform in ±0.3; η = 0.1; weights saturate at ±12. The outputs' Φ is the Teacher setting, t − o, with the world's target; a hidden unit's Φ is Lab 7's `passed-back`, its slope times the weighted sum of the outputs' Φ; every change is computed before any weight moves, and every write is `learnWeights` with its baseline treated as the weight on an input that is always 1. **The youth**: the old world's 24 (thing, relation) patterns, each stored in the fast store and replayed interleaved in a seeded order, 600 passes (14,400 presentations; 161 ms headless, paid in slices of 5 passes on screen). **The new season**: the acorn tree and the weasel; replay off is 1,000 passes over the 6 new patterns (6,000 presentations), replay on the same number of presentations drawn from the 30 patterns interleaved. **Damage**: noise on each hidden unit's net input, Gaussian with sd 3 × the slider's value, drawn from the network's own stream, applied only when the network is read and never when it learns; the property table reads 20 draws; the forager reads one draw per log per decision. The `trainedSlowLearner(seed)` the panels and foragers share is cached per seed and its damage reset after every read.

**The four foragers** (`foragers/foragers.ts`), each over the student's own blocks (the same plan, clearing and starting state; its own digs, so its own refill history), walking straight at the range pace (`SCRIPT_PACE`, 1.5 m/s) to the log it chooses, back to the nest when called: **Usual only** (the slow learner's *has beetles under it* for the kind, the list for tonight), **Tendency only** (the tendency unit, the list), **Tonight too** (the slow learner, the store at strong separation, the list), **Tonight too, nights blurred** (the store at weak). Damage: `{ noStore }` and `{ slowNoise }`.

## 7. Where the build departed from the spec, or where its targets turned out wrong

1. **The night is 55 s, not about 2 minutes** (§8.3). At the range pace with 3 s digs and 3 s meals, a 2-minute night lets a scripted perfect rememberer empty all seven loose-bark logs; the "about four of seven" target holds at 55 s. A slower scripted pace barely changes it (§4.1). If a student at the keys empties only two logs in 55 s on the walk, the constants to move are `NIGHT_S`, `DIG_S`, `EAT_S` (`logs/night.ts`) and `SCRIPT_PACE` (`logs/scripted.ts`), and §4.1 is the sweep to re-run.
2. **The Usual-only target holds on night 2 and not on night 3** (§14: "about two logs emptied the night before on nights 2 and 3"): 1.9 on night 2, 1.1 on night 3, because a forager that digs at random empties about two logs on night 2, so only about two are "emptied last night" on night 3. Both targets cannot hold together at any length (§4.1); reported, not retuned.
3. **The distinct nights share at most one feature value with the look-alike nights, never the ground** (§8.4, §14: "none matches the look-alike nights"). With three moons, three weathers and three sounds, three nights that differ on every feature cannot all avoid the look-alike value on each.
4. **The forager acts on the retrieved strength** (§9.3 says the panel shows "the logs the forager will treat as emptied last night"). With the forager's cue being what last night was like, the stored night of last night always has the larger share, so a threshold at 0.5 never errs and the store showed no confusion at any setting; and with a cue of tonight's input, confusions came from code noise and were no lower on the distinct block. Treating a log as emptied as often as the store's answer says is the overlap doing the work, with no rate written in; `structure.test.ts` holds that no threshold on the blend exists. The map answers follow the same rule.
5. **The forager holds what last night was like**, its features and context, as one register overwritten at each dawn, and cues the store with it. Nothing else the forager has tonight says which stored night was last night; the register is a fixed-size state, not a record of what was emptied, which stays the store's.
6. **Usual only chooses at random among loose-bark logs**, not nearest first: a forager with no basis for preferring one loose-bark log to another is given none, and with nearest-first it would redig the logs it emptied the night before as surely as Tendency only does (§5).
7. **The new season's loss is in the specific properties** (§9.4: "old properties fall well below their starting level"). The proportion of all answers right stays at 0.85 to 0.92 with replay off, because most answers are the many properties a thing does not have; the properties the things have fall to 0.43 (specific) while general ones stay near 1.0. The plot shows properties still said yes, by level and together, which is what the reading's damaged network lost in order.
8. **Damage is measured on the properties the things have** for the same reason: by the proportion of all answers right, general properties looked lost first, since a specific property's "no" is right seven times in eight whatever the network does.
9. **The separation stage's settings and the context's weight and drift were chosen by sweep** (§4.3; `measure.probe.ts`), so that the slider spans a range on the look-alike block while the distinct block is nearly clean by the medium setting.
10. **Part 2's unit ids are `p2-first` and `p2-second`**, and its store names the conditions by order, so that no identifier on the Open tab's rows could name organization before Part 1's reveal.
11. **The practice clearing is Lab 9's nest with Lab 9's landmarks**; the other three are new. The spec's four clearings share one geometry; here the practice clearing has its own, since Lab 9's landmarks already stand there.
12. **The remember and know definitions on the pre-reveal screen are paraphrased**; the source's sentences contain *associated*, a forbidden substring before the reveal, and are printed on the reveal.
13. **The instruction screens say "What to have on hand"**, not "ready": *ready* is a word on the rough list.
14. **A fourth value of a feature was not added** to make distinct nights avoid the look-alike nights entirely (item 3); recorded for Jon.
15. **Nothing was cut.** Export and Load, the order of output, the new season's two runs, the similarity matrix, sound, the split of repeat digs, and the free orbit of the room are all built.
16. **A baby's rattle and a violin bow are not in the room** (Jon's examples): no CC0 model exists in the kits or on Poly Haven. The fire extinguisher and the garden trowel are Poly Haven's, at 1.6 and 2.0 MB; the fire extinguisher carries Korean lettering (`korean_fire_extinguisher_01`).
